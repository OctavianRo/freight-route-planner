How routes are estimated

Each trip starts with the oldest available order. Remaining capacity goes to nearby orders, with an adjustable preference for older dates. Each additional stop must fit both the payload and the road-distance budget, including the return to your depot.

Distances are driving estimates between postcode district or area centres, including for full postcodes. Use these trips for load planning. Routing uses a car profile. Confirm delivery addresses and HGV access before dispatch. The heuristic can produce a longer route or more loads than the mathematical optimum.
