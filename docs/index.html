How routes are estimated

Each trip starts with the oldest available order. Remaining capacity goes to nearby orders, with an adjustable preference for older dates. A route improvement pass shortens the proposed stop sequence from and back to your depot.

Distances are straight-line estimates between postcode district or area centres, including for full postcodes. Use these trips for load planning. Confirm road distances, delivery addresses and HGV access before dispatch. The heuristic can produce a longer route or more loads than the mathematical optimum.
